## Decision records

- [ ] Fictional demonstration scope and audience accepted; simulated services clearly understood.
- [ ] Proposed role matrix reviewed; synthetic grants distinguished from approved hospital assignments.
- [ ] A–J workflow findings documented, including unsupported features and usability issues.
- [ ] Paid discovery deliverables, quotation inputs and commercial process agreed separately.
- [ ] Dedicated synthetic integration target approved by exact identity and purpose.
- [ ] Hosted schema/history, actual Auth/REST/RLS, recovery and specific execution approval verified before any hosted migration.
- [ ] Clinical governance checklist signed by hospital leadership before clinical use.
- [ ] Security, privacy, hosting, support and live-data pilot approval independently recorded.

All boxes begin unchecked. The repository's local tests do not sign hospital acceptance. Record decision, owner, date, versioned evidence and review date externally or in an approved governance register, without secrets or patient-level content.
